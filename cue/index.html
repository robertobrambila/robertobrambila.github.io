Hidden from screen sharing

Write the talk once, and give each line a cue: the few words that bring it back.

Present, and every line collapses to its cue. Hover one, and the line comes back.

Share your screen, and the room sees only your slide. Cue stays out of the share.

Rehearse with less each run: first letters, then cues, then nothing at all.

Features

From first draft to speech day.

  1. 01

    Write it once.

    • Paste a script, and Cue splits it into thoughts.
    • Give each one a cue, or let Cue suggest one.
    • Bring in speaker notes from PowerPoint.
  2. 02

    Get off book.

    • Rehearse with a little less on screen each run.
    • First letters, then cues, then nothing at all.
    • Track recall and personal bests to speech day.
  3. 03

    A coach you choose.

    • After a run: what worked, and what to practice.
    • Use GitHub Copilot, a local model with Ollama, or your own OpenAI or Anthropic key.
    • Transcribed on your Mac. Review before it’s sent.
  4. 04

    Present from a glance.

    • Lines collapse to cues. Hover to peek, click to pin.
    • Open a whole page at once with ⌘E.
    • A big title on each page for its key message.
  5. 05

    Keep your place.

    • Cue can follow your PowerPoint or Keynote deck.
    • Someone else driving? Say “next slide.”
    • Shortcuts work while your slides are in front.
  6. 06

    Only you see it.

    • Cue stays out of screen shares and recordings.
    • Place it beside Teams or under your camera.
    • A small indicator confirms it’s hidden.
